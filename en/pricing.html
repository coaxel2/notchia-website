Frequently asked

What is free in NotchIA?

Essential, free forever: multi-source media with lyrics, calendar and reminders, system HUD, Sneak Peek, detections, full Concentration and visual customization. No payment or account required.

What happens if I stop paying?

Pro features (live AI, file management, clipboard history) are switched off. The rest of NotchIA keeps working normally.

How many Macs can I install it on?

Pro monthly covers 1 Mac, Pro lifetime 2 Macs. The free Essential plan needs no license and installs on as many Macs as you like.

Does Lifetime include future major versions?

Yes. All NotchIA updates, including major versions, are included for lifetime buyers.

I lost my license key. What now?

The /account page lets you retrieve a key from the purchase email.
