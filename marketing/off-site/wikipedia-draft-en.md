'''NotchIA''' is an independent macOS utility developed by Axel Courty. The app turns the MacBook notch, or a virtual menu-bar area on Macs without a physical notch, into an interactive control center for recurring macOS workflows.<ref>NotchIA, official website, https://notchia.app/</ref>

## History

NotchIA is distributed as a macOS app outside the Mac App Store. The official website lists macOS 15.0 or later as the minimum requirement, with support for both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.<ref>NotchIA, “Features”, https://notchia.app/features</ref> Version 2.8.0, named “Wise Owl”, was announced on May 15, 2026. It introduced Digest, an RSS/Atom feed summary feature generated locally with Apple Intelligence, and document summaries in the app's Shelf module.<ref>Axel Courty, “2.8.0 Wise Owl”, NotchIA, May 15, 2026, https://notchia.app/blog/wise-owl-2-8-0</ref>

## Features

The app includes several modules: multi-source media controls, calendar and reminders, a focus timer, live status for development AI assistants, a file Shelf, file conversion, clipboard history, and system HUD replacement. Apple Intelligence features, including Digest and document summaries, require macOS 26 or a compatible version with Apple's Foundation Models.<ref>NotchIA, “Features”, https://notchia.app/features</ref>

## Business Model

NotchIA uses a freemium model. The Essential tier is free. The Pro tier is available either as a monthly subscription or as a lifetime one-time purchase for two Macs, according to the pricing published by the developer.<ref>NotchIA, “Pricing”, https://notchia.app/pricing</ref>
