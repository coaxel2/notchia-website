# Business terms — questions to clarify with Setapp before signing

These are the open points we want answered (and where useful, negotiated) before we sign a Vendor Agreement.

## 1. Revenue share

- What is the current vendor split? (Public references suggest ~ 70% to vendor / 30% to Setapp on attributable revenue — please confirm 2026 terms.)
- Is the split calculated on:
  - **Monthly active users (MAU)** of NotchIA among Setapp subscribers, or
  - **Total usage time** in NotchIA vs the rest of the Setapp catalog, or
  - A **hybrid weighting** (some apps weighted higher by category)?
- Is there a **minimum activation threshold** (e.g. user must launch the app ≥ N times in a month for revenue to count)?
- How is **first-time install vs returning user** weighted, if at all?

## 2. Exclusivity

- Does Setapp require **exclusivity** for the Pro tier? Specifically:
  - Can we keep the **Essentiel (free) tier** available for direct download on notchia.app? (Critical for our funnel: free tier feeds Product Hunt / SEO / Homebrew install and converts later.)
  - Can we keep the **Pro Lifetime (€24.99 one-time)** sold direct on notchia.app for users outside Setapp's covered countries or who prefer a perpetual license? Open to making it Setapp-priority (e.g. discount inside Setapp, full price outside).
  - Can we keep the **Pro Monthly (€2.99/mo)** direct subscription, or does Setapp require we pull it from direct sale?
- If exclusivity is required on **one** SKU, our preference order: 1) drop Pro Monthly direct sale, 2) keep Pro Lifetime + Essentiel direct.

## 3. Payout schedule

- What is the payout frequency — monthly, quarterly, on a NET-30 / NET-60 basis?
- What is the **minimum payout threshold** before funds are wired?
- Which **currencies** are supported (EUR preferred for a France-based vendor)?
- Is there a **vendor portal** with real-time analytics on MAU, attributable revenue and payout forecast?
- How are **VAT / EU invoicing** handled — does Setapp issue a self-billing invoice to the vendor, or does the vendor invoice Setapp?

## 4. Marketing & visibility

- What marketing support is included for newly listed apps:
  - Featured slot in the Setapp app on launch week?
  - Newsletter mention to the existing subscriber base?
  - Setapp.com homepage feature?
  - Social posts (X, LinkedIn, Mastodon, YouTube short)?
- Is there a **launch-window co-marketing budget** we can co-fund (e.g. paid social, press push)?
- What is the **ranking algorithm** inside the Setapp app — purely usage-based, or curated? Is there anything we can do to maintain visibility beyond launch month?
- Are **roundup / collection placements** (e.g. "Best apps for developers", "Notch utilities") editorial picks or pay-to-play?

## 5. Catalog positioning

- Setapp already lists NotchNook in the notch-utility space. How does the catalog handle **two apps in the same category** — do you prefer differentiated positioning, or is direct comparison encouraged in the listing copy?
- Are we free to position NotchIA as the **AI developer cockpit** angle, or does Setapp prefer the more neutral "notch utility / menu-bar" framing?

## 6. Updates & release control

- Once listed, what is the **review SLA for app updates**? (Critical — we patch Claude Code log-format changes within 48 h; we need an equally fast Setapp build channel.)
- Can we ship **hot-fixes** outside the standard review cycle for security or breakage issues?
- Is there a **beta channel** for Setapp users so we can pre-test updates with willing subscribers before rolling broadly?

## 7. Localisation & EU specifics

- NotchIA is localised in FR / EN / ES / DE. Will the Setapp listing surface the localised metadata automatically, or do we submit one set?
- Any specifics on **EU DSA / Digital Markets Act** compliance Setapp expects from vendors (transparency reports, takedown handling, etc.)?

## 8. Termination & exit

- What is the **notice period** if either party wants to end the partnership?
- On termination, what happens to **active Setapp subscribers** who installed NotchIA — do they get a grace period, a discount on direct purchase, or a hard cut-off?
- Can we **export the list of users** who installed NotchIA via Setapp (with their consent) for migration purposes?

## 9. Apple notarisation (link with 04-technical-spec.md)

- NotchIA is currently **signed but not Apple-notarised**. We can complete notarisation as a condition of acceptance — please confirm if it's required and any specific entitlement constraints Setapp wants reviewed.

## 10. Timeline

- What's the realistic timeline from this email to (a) acceptance decision, (b) integration kickoff, (c) live listing?
- Who is the **single point of contact** on the Setapp side for the integration phase?
