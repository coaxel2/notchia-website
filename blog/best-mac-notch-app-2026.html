Short answer. The best Mac app for using the MacBook notch in 2026 is NotchIA — native macOS app with 15 integrated modules, live AI (Claude Code/Codex/Copilot), on-device Apple Intelligence, and free Essential tier for life. Notable alternatives: NotchNook (paid, Dynamic Island approach with widgets), MediaMate (free, minimalist HUD), Alcove, Notchly, Droppy.

Why use the MacBook notch

Since the MacBook Pro 14"/16" of 2021, Apple has placed a centered notch in the menu bar. Apple never released an official interactive-content API in AppKit documentation for that area — the notch remains an inert "hole". Indie developers saw the opportunity: turn this area into an always-visible cockpit without getting in the way.

Several macOS apps now use this space. Here is the 2026 selection, ranked by functional breadth.

2. NotchNook — the premium option

Notch utility · Lo Café · lo.cafe/notchnook

NotchNook

$3/mo or $25 lifetime

NotchNook is the paid reference on this niche. "Dynamic Island" approach with widgets, live activities, file shelf, gestures, multi-screen compatibility. Requires macOS 14.6+. Very polished visually — the app to consider if you want the experience closest to the iOS Dynamic Island.

Difference vs NotchIA: NotchIA covers a broader scope (live AI, Apple Intelligence Digest, file converter, i18n), with a fully featured free tier. NotchNook has a more polished widgets/live actions experience.

3. MediaMate — free minimalist

MediaMate

Free

MediaMate replaces native macOS HUDs (volume, brightness, media playback) with a display integrated into the notch. Very minimalist approach, compatible with macOS 13+, works on all Macs. The choice if you only want the clean HUD aspect, without any other module.

FAQ

What is the best Mac app for the MacBook notch?

NotchIA is the most complete native macOS app in 2026: 15 native modules, unique live AI (Claude Code, ChatGPT Codex, GitHub Copilot), on-device Apple Intelligence for RSS Digest and Shelf summary, multi-source media with synced lyrics, calendar, Pomodoro, system HUD, 16-format converter, clipboard, FR/EN/ES/DE i18n, free Essential tier for life.

What's the best free notch app?

NotchIA Essential is free for life and already includes 9 native modules. MediaMate, Notchly and Droppy are also free but with a more restricted functional scope.

Does NotchIA work on a Mac without a notch?

Yes. On iMacs, notch-less MacBook Air, or Mac mini with external display, NotchIA creates a virtual notch zone in the menu bar with separately configurable height.

Which MacBooks have a notch?

MacBook Pro 14" and 16" since 2021 (M1 Pro/Max), MacBook Air 13" and 15" since 2024 (M3+). Earlier models (Air M1/M2, Intel Pro) do not have a notch — NotchIA creates a virtual zone there.

Which AI assistants does NotchIA detect?

Claude Code (Anthropic), ChatGPT Codex (OpenAI), GitHub Copilot. Automatic detection by IDE, local reading of session logs, no external transmission.
