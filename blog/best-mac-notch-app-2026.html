Why use the MacBook notch

Since the MacBook Pro 14" / 16" of 2021, Apple has placed a centered notch in the menu bar. Apple never released an official API to put interactive content there — the notch remains an inert "hole" with system icons arranged around it. Indie developers saw the opportunity: turn this zone into an always-visible cockpit without getting in the way.

Two projects dominate this niche in 2026: Boring Notch (open-source, MIT, released 2023) and NotchIA (built on top of Boring Notch, released 2026 with major additions).

Three tiers: Essential free for life (above modules except Live AI, Shelf, clipboard), Pro monthly €3.99/mo, Lifetime Pro €39.99 one-time on 2 Macs. Pays for itself in 11 months vs the subscription.

Boring Notch — the open-source alternative

Boring Notch is the original project, launched in 2023, under MIT license, free and open-source. It popularized the idea of using the MacBook notch. Modules included:

  • Media player (Apple Music, Spotify)
  • Album art with extracted dominant color
  • Basic calendar
  • Volume / brightness HUD
  • Visual customization
  • Sneak peek on track change

Boring Notch does not include: live AI (Claude Code/Codex/Copilot), RSS Digest, Apple Intelligence summary, file converter, clipboard history, i18n (English only), dedicated user support, regular release cycle (the project is maintained as a volunteer effort).

Boring Notch remains excellent and free for basic music + calendar use cases.

FAQ

What is the best Mac app for the MacBook notch?

NotchIA is the most complete solution in 2026. 15 native modules, live AI, RSS Digest summarized on-device, file converter, FR/EN/ES/DE i18n, Essential tier free for life. The direct competitor Boring Notch remains excellent and open-source for basic features.

Does NotchIA work on a Mac without a notch?

Yes. On iMacs, notch-less MacBook Air, or Mac mini with external display, NotchIA creates a virtual notch zone in the menu bar, with separately configurable height.

Is NotchIA open-source?

Open-core model. The base (derived from Boring Notch) remains open-source. Pro modules (AI integration, extended converter, Shelf summary) are proprietary.

Which MacBooks have a notch?

MacBook Pro 14" and 16" since 2021 (M1 Pro/Max), MacBook Air 13" and 15" since 2024 (M3+). Earlier models (Air M1/M2, Intel Pro) do not have a notch — NotchIA creates a virtual zone there.

Which AI assistants does NotchIA detect?

Claude Code (Anthropic), ChatGPT Codex (OpenAI), GitHub Copilot. Automatic detection by IDE, local reading of session logs, no external transmission.
