TopNotch alternative

If you're looking for a TopNotch alternative on Mac, NotchIA is the exploit-the-notch option: it turns the notch into a productivity cockpit with Claude Code/Codex tracking, on-device Apple Intelligence Digest, media, calendar, Shelf, Focus and system HUD.

What's missing in TopNotch?

  • TopNotch hides the notch instead of turning it into a workflow surface.
  • No modules for media, calendar, AI, files or focus.
  • No live Claude Code or Codex tracking.

How NotchIA solves it

  • Local tracking for Claude Code and ChatGPT Codex with 10 states, tokens, cache and 5h/7d quotas.
  • RSS Digest and PDF summaries via Apple Intelligence Foundation Models on macOS 26+, with graceful fallback on macOS 15.
  • Daily modules in the same notch: media, calendar, Shelf, Convert, Focus, clipboard and system HUD.

In daily use, NotchIA acts as a passive surface: you do not open another window to know whether an AI assistant is compiling, reading a file, waiting for permission or consuming quota. The same space can then show music, the next calendar event, a Focus timer, a file in the Shelf or a volume/brightness HUD. That breadth is what makes the alternative different from a simple visual notch layer.

The philosophy stays honest: NotchIA is not trying to replace every specialized utility. It focuses on signals that deserve to stay visible without interrupting work, with a local-first base and no telemetry. For Claude Code or Codex users, live assistant tracking is the feature that changes the value of the notch most.

When TopNotch still makes sense

TopNotch still makes sense when you dislike the MacBook notch visually and simply want the menu bar to look black and uniform.
